Frequently asked

Does Scare Stream play movies?

No. It shows you where a title is streaming and links you to that service, where you watch it. Availability covers the US.

Why is a movie or show missing?

The catalog comes from TMDB and JustWatch, so only titles with known US streaming availability appear. Pull down to refresh on the Tonight screen, and check that your services are selected on the Services tab.

Why doesn't my service appear in the list?

Scare Stream includes the major US streaming services supported by TMDB's data. Rentals and purchases are hidden when "Only show titles included with my subscriptions" is on.

Where is my watchlist stored?

Entirely on your device. There's no account, no sign-up and no tracking. Deleting the app removes your watchlist, watched history and preferences.

How do I reset my recommendations?

Your taste profile is derived from what you open, save and mark watched. Deleting and reinstalling the app resets it.
