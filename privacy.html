Privacy Policy — Scare Stream

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Scare Stream ("the app", "we", "us") is a horror movie and series discovery app. This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: we do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

The app saves the following preferences and history locally on your device (using iOS standard storage). This data never leaves your device unless you choose to back up your device with Apple:

  • The streaming services you selected (e.g. Netflix, Shudder)
  • Your watchlist and titles marked as watched
  • Your filter choices (moods, scare level, included-only)
  • A cached copy of the movie catalog and movie details to make the app fast and to work offline
  • A taste profile derived from what you open, watchlist, and mark watched

Deleting the app removes all of this data.

Network connections the app makes

The app itself has no server and sends nothing to us. To show you current horror titles, artwork, and streaming availability, the app connects directly to these third-party services:

  • TMDB (The Movie Database) — movie/series metadata, artwork, and trailer information. Privacy policy: themoviedb.org/privacy-policy
  • JustWatch (via TMDB) — streaming availability data. Privacy policy: justwatch.com/privacy-policy
  • YouTube (Google) — when you play a trailer, the trailer is loaded in an embedded YouTube player. Google may handle data per its own policies: policies.google.com/privacy

The app does not contain ads, analytics, tracking, or sign-in, and it does not use your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Notifications

If you turn on the weekly reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device (every Friday evening). Local notifications are handled entirely by iOS — no notification data is sent to or through any server.

Children

Scare Stream is a horror-themed app and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or stored by us, there is nothing for us to access, export, or delete on your behalf. If you have questions, contact us at thaxtonjd@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds data collection (for example accounts or analytics), this policy will be updated before that version ships.
